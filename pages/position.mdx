<h3 className="text-gray-800 text-3xl font-semibold sm:text-4xl">
            Join ARC Lab
          </h3>
ARC Lab welcomes students, researchers, clinicians, engineers, and professionals from diverse academic backgrounds who are interested in responsible AI for health. By responsible AI, we mean AI that is developed and used in ways that are safe, equitable, transparent, accountable, respectful of privacy, and responsive to the needs of the people and settings it is intended to serve. We are especially interested in those who want to work on real-world health questions using AI, multimodal data, statistical methods, and related computational approaches.

We do not expect every applicant to have the same training. Relevant backgrounds may include computer science, engineering, data science, statistics, health informatics, public health, medicine, or related fields. Depending on the project, useful experience may include machine learning, multimodal AI, natural language processing, causal methods, optimization, data analysis, or scientific programming.

If your interests connect with our work, please contact `m`[dot]`noaeen`[@]`utoronto`[dot]`ca` and visit the **Research** page to learn more about current areas of work. We may not be able to respond to every message. If you do not receive a response, it generally means that your background does not currently align with an available project. We encourage you to check back for future opportunities.

ARC Lab is accepting applications for the Fall 2027 intake. The application deadline is expected in November 2026.

### What We Look For
- **Proven research experience** shown through publications, conference papers, or substantial projects  
- **Exceptional academic writing skills** and the ability to explain complex ideas clearly  
- **Strong discipline and time-management** to meet project milestones and publication deadlines  
- **Interest in grant writing** or experience preparing funding proposals  
- **Technical depth** in at least one of the following: machine learning, natural language processing, data science, or information visualization  
- Ability to work **independently and as part of an interdisciplinary team**  


### How to Apply
1. **CV and unofficial transcripts**    
2. **Writing sample** (published paper, thesis chapter, or technical report)    

Email your application as a single PDF to **m[dot]noaeen[at]utoronto[dot]ca**.
