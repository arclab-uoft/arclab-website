Dua Fayaz is a student, researcher, and community advocate with interests spanning healthcare, biomedical research, public health, and emerging health technologies. Her experiences have taken her from clinical and academic research environments to community-based initiatives focused on making healthcare and essential resources more accessible.

Dua has contributed to research across areas including pediatric kidney transplantation and artificial intelligence validation. Her growing interest in responsible AI and digital health has also led her to explore how emerging technologies can be evaluated and implemented in ways that are evidence-based, transparent, and trustworthy.

Beyond research, Dua is passionate about addressing barriers that affect underserved and newcomer communities. She has helped co-found community initiatives focused on healthcare navigation, newcomer settlement, and maternal and newborn support, contributing to program development, grant-funded projects, educational outreach, and resource distribution.

Across her work, Dua is particularly interested in the intersection of medicine, research, technology, and health equity. She hopes to pursue a career in medicine while continuing to contribute to research and community initiatives that translate knowledge into meaningful improvements in people's lives.
