Sindi is an interdisciplinary scholar blending mathematics, statistics, and biomedical science to solve complex challenges in medicine. Currently an Ontario Graduate Scholar at the University of Toronto’s IHPME, she is pursuing a M.Sc. in Health Systems Artificial Intelligence under the co-supervision of Dr. Pablo Galvez-Hernandez and Dr. Mohammad Noaeen. Sindi’s diverse research background spans both dry and wet lab environments. She has developed simulation studies to predict epidemic underreporting at the University of Guelph and investigated stem cell-derived brain-in-a-dish models at SickKids-PGCRL. Driven by a deep curiosity about human biology, shaped by hands-on anatomical training at the University of Guelph's premier cadaver lab, she now uses AI to optimize the delivery of healthcare and alleviate human suffering.
