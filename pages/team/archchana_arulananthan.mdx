Archchana is a research assistant at ARCLabs focused on applying machine learning to complex healthcare problems. She brings a dual foundation in clinical science and biomedical research, currently a Physician Assistant in training at McMaster University, with a prior HBSc in Immunology and Molecular Biology from the University of Toronto. Her clinical research experience spans Unity Health Toronto and Grace Health Centre, where she worked across digital health initiatives and patient data management. This work gave her hands-on exposure to electronic medical records, real-world clinical datasets, and emerging healthcare AI tools, informing a grounded perspective on the practical constraints and opportunities ML faces at the point of care. At ARCLabs, Archchana bridges clinical domain knowledge and data-driven methods to support research at the intersection of AI and health systems with a particular interest in how models can be designed and validated for real-world clinical utility.
