Alex Chen holds a bachelor's degree in Computer Science with a minor in Statistics and is currently pursuing an MSc in Biostatistics at the Dalla Lana School of Public Health, University of Toronto. His background spans algorithm design, statistical modelling, and applied machine learning, with experience in neural networks, multimodal large language models, and clustering methods. Under the joint supervision of Dr. Zahra Shakeri and Dr. Mohammad Noaeen, his current research focuses on improving the transportability of multimodal atrial fibrillation classifiers across healthcare populations.
