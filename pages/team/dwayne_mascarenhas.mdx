Dwayne Mascarenhas is a staff neonatologist at Mount Sinai Hospital in Toronto and an Assistant Professor in the Department of Pediatrics at the University of Toronto. He is also pursuing a PhD at the Institute of Medical Science, University of Toronto. His clinical and research work focuses on neonatal medicine, with a particular interest in the application of multimodal artificial intelligence in neonatal intensive care. His work brings together clinical expertise, neonatal research, and emerging computational methods to support better understanding, monitoring, and decision making in the NICU.
