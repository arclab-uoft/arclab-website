Mohammed AboArab is a PhD graduate whose research focuses on computational and physics-informed approaches for complex biomedical systems. During his work with ARC Lab, he studied neural operators for image-based vascular hemodynamics, with an emphasis on combining physical knowledge and machine learning for modelling biological flow systems. His broader interests include physics-informed AI, scientific machine learning, neural operators, simulation, and reliable computational methods for biomedical applications.
