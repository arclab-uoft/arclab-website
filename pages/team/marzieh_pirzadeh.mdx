Marzieh Pirzadeh, MD, is a physician and clinical researcher with a strong background in translational medicine, clinical research, and evidence-based medicine. Her work has contributed to more than 27 peer-reviewed publications across ophthalmology, immunology, oncology, dermatology, and neuroscience.
She currently practices in a palliative care unit in Tehran, integrating evidence-based medicine with compassionate patient care. Her research expertise includes clinical study design, systematic reviews and meta-analyses, biostatistics, scientific writing, and molecular laboratory techniques.
