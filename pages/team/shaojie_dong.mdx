Shaojie Dong works at the intersection of artificial intelligence, data science, and public health. His research interests include health AI, precision public health, causal machine learning, and environmental exposure modelling. He is currently working under the supervision of Dr. Kuan Liu and Dr. Mohammad Noaeen on the development of an Ontario specific daily PM2.5 exposure product using machine learning models.
