Muhammad Tayyab Zamir is a PhD researcher and data scientist working in natural language processing, computer vision, multimodal AI, and generative AI. His research focuses on integrating medical imaging, radiology reports, and heterogeneous health data using deep learning and large language models to develop robust and explainable AI systems. He also has experience in scalable machine learning, 3D modelling, and end to end AI pipelines, with applications across healthcare and intelligent systems.
