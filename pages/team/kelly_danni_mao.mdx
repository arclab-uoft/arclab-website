Danni (Kelly) Mao is an undergraduate student at the University of Toronto specializing in Statistical Science. She has experience applying statistical and machine-learning methods to projects involving microbiome, health, and other real-world data. Her research interests include artificial intelligence, biostatistics, genomics, mental health, and data-driven approaches to understanding diseases and addressing healthcare research questions. She is interested in exploring how reliable AI methods can support evidence-based research and healthcare. Outside of research, she enjoys creating mods for Stardew Valley, combining her interests in programming and design.
