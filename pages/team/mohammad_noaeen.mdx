Dr. Mohammad Noaeen is an Assistant Professor of Health Informatics in the Institute of Health Policy, Management and Evaluation at the Dalla Lana School of Public Health, University of Toronto. He leads the AI for Responsible Care (ARC) Lab, where his work bridges health informatics, artificial intelligence, statistical learning, environmental health, and transportation systems. His research examines how diverse sources of data can be used to better understand health risks, support precision public health, and improve decision-making for individuals, communities, and health systems.

Dr. Noaeen’s work draws on multimodal AI, causal and statistical methods, optimization, and data-driven modelling to address problems at the intersection of health, environment, mobility, and equity. He received his PhD in Computer Engineering from the University of Calgary and completed postdoctoral training at Harvard Business School and at the University of Toronto’s Institute of Health Policy, Management and Evaluation and Dalla Lana School of Public Health.
